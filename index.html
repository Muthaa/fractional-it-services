What does “fractional IT” mean?

You engage me for an agreed amount of technical support instead of hiring a full-time IT employee. The scope can include maintenance, user support and improvements, based on your priorities.

Can you work with our existing hosting or IT provider?

Yes. Work can be scoped around your current setup, with responsibilities and access agreed before changes. Where a provider needs to act, I can help identify and explain the issue.

Do you offer emergency or 24/7 support?

Availability and response times are agreed for each engagement. This service does not include an automatic 24/7 response commitment.

Will I keep control of my accounts and systems?

Your business should retain control of its domains, hosting and service accounts. We agree on the access needed for the job and include relevant documentation in the handover.
